# Live Ops Strategy

## Why Companions Are the Live Ops Vehicle

Companions are content that can be shipped indefinitely:
- New archetype = new story, new voice, new agenda, new betrayal
- Each companion takes 2-4 weeks to design and voice
- They slot into existing zones and systems
- Players who've completed the base campaign have a reason to return

## Seasonal Companion Model

### How it works
- Each season (~3 months) introduces one new companion
- Season 1: Brann, Lyra, Ossian, The Echo (base game)
- Season 2: The Dockhand (a spy), The Alchemist (experimenter)
- Season 3: Community vote — top-voted archetype concept gets built

### Season structure
- Launch: new companion available to all players
- Week 2: companion's unique questline unlocks
- Week 6: companion's hidden agenda becomes resolvable for the first time
- Week 10: "season finale" — scripted betrayal or resolution arc
- End of season: companion becomes permanently available in the roster

## Community-Authored Companions

Phase 5 goal: a curated system where community members can submit companion concepts.

**Submission format:**
- 250-word concept pitch
- 3 sample dialogue lines
- One hidden agenda idea

**Curation:**
- Narrative team reviews weekly
- Top 5 per month enter a public vote
- Winning concept is developed by the narrative team (community author is credited)

**Why curated, not open:**
- LLM characters with bad personality prompts are actively annoying
- Quality bar matters more here than in most user-generated content
- We want the community to feel heard, not to ship unpolished content

## Recurring Seasonal Campaign

Each season can include a short (2-3 hour) seasonal campaign:
- New zone, seasonal enemy faction, boss with unique gimmick
- Season-exclusive loot (cosmetic; no pay-to-win)
- Seasonal companions have unique reactions to seasonal content
- Campaign is permanent after season ends; seasonal exclusives are cosmetic only

## Anti-Churn Design

### "Your companion remembers you"
Players who return after months away hear from their companion:
- "I wasn't sure you were coming back."
- "The town's changed since you left."
- References to the last thing they did together

This is stored in semantic facts + a simple timestamp check.

### Campaign carry-over
A player who completed Act 1 with Brann and left can return and continue.
Brann remembers. The campaign state is there. Trust where they left it.

### New character, same companion
Players creating alts can "introduce" their previous companion to their new character.
The companion has opinions about the new character based on what the first character told them.
This requires designing carefully but creates powerful cross-play moments.
