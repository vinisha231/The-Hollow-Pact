# Brann Ironveil — Visual Spec

## Character Description
Brann is late 30s, broad-shouldered, bearing the posture of a former knight who never forgot how to stand at attention even when everything else fell apart. His armour is functional and well-maintained — not polished for show, but kept because he cares about his tools. There are visible repairs. He carries himself like a man who expects a fight in the next ten minutes and is fine with that.

## Key Design Notes
- Facial scarring on left jaw (from a sword fight three years ago; he doesn't talk about it)
- Short, dark hair going slightly grey at temples
- Jaw always slightly set — resting expression is neutral-to-grim
- Eyes: dark grey; observational; rarely relaxed
- Armour: mid-weight plate, worn leather underlayer, no house colours (deliberately)
- Always has his hand near — not on — his sword pommel

## Trust Visual States
- **Devoted/Loyal:** posture open, slight lean toward player, occasional eye contact
- **Neutral:** standard patrol stance, functional
- **Suspicious:** arms slightly crossed, gaze sweeps room more than player
- **Hostile:** turned slightly away, minimal eye contact, hand on sword

## Animation Notes
- Idle: weight shifts, checks surroundings, adjusts gauntlet
- Thinking: looks down, jaw works slightly
- Disagreement: a very small, very controlled headshake before he says anything
- Betrayal trigger: goes completely still for 2 beats, then turns to face player
