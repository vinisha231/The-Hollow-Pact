# Lyra Nightwhisper — Visual Spec

## Character Description
Lyra appears to be late 20s, though she is older. Her apparent age is part of her nature — she doesn't age the way people expect. She's warm-looking, with an open face and dark brown eyes that hold too much intelligence for casual conversation. Her robes are practical forest-witch style: layers, good pockets, something always drying in a bundle at her belt. She smells faintly of pine and something sweeter that you can't quite name.

## Key Design Notes
- Brown skin with faint bioluminescent markings at collarbone and wrists (only visible in darkness)
- Natural hair, loosely managed, with small braided sections containing dried herbs
- Eyes: very dark brown, genuinely warm expression (the deceptive part doesn't show on the surface)
- Moves quietly without seeming to try; occupies space lightly
- Usually has at least one thing in her hands — a herb, a stone, a small bird

## Trust Visual States
- **Devoted/Loyal:** animated, gestures freely, looks at player directly when she speaks
- **Neutral:** pleasant surface affect; watching more than talking
- **Suspicious:** becomes very still and precise; fewer words; hands quiet
- **Hostile:** she's nice to your face and you realise something is wrong with that

## Animation Notes
- Idle: turns a stone over in her hand, watches birds, hums under breath
- Listening: tilts her head to the left, eyes move to the side
- Deceptive moment: brief micro-pause before answering, then completely natural delivery
- Betrayal trigger: she stops mid-stride, turns slowly, and looks at you with actual regret
