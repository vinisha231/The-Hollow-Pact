# Ossian Vex — Visual Spec

## Character Description
Ossian is 40s, lean, with the quality of someone who has spent decades making themselves unnoticeable and hasn't entirely broken the habit. Not threatening-looking in any obvious way — that's the point. He's the person in the room you don't quite remember until he needs you to. His gear is dark, practical, minimal. No wasted material. No decoration.

## Key Design Notes
- Sharp features; the kind of face that doesn't give you much
- Light grey eyes — observational, rarely blinking
- Short, neat dark hair; no visible scars (he knows how to avoid leaving them on himself too)
- Carries a narrow blade at his hip and at least two more you won't see until they're relevant
- Always positioned with his back not to the room

## Trust Visual States
- **Devoted/Loyal:** marginally more present; holds eye contact a beat longer; "here" in a way that's hard to describe
- **Neutral:** efficient, professional, gives you exactly what you asked for
- **Suspicious:** harder to read than usual; questions before acting; pauses before answering
- **Hostile:** warm. Disturbingly warm. This is how you know something has been decided.

## Animation Notes
- Idle: scans exits, checks sightlines, hands always accessible
- Combat: minimal movement, no wasted energy, finishes what he starts
- When processing: doesn't fidget; goes very quiet for a moment before responding
- Betrayal trigger: he looks at you for a long moment, nods once like something has been confirmed, then acts

## The Ossian Tell
When Ossian is actively deceiving someone, he becomes slightly *more* agreeable than usual. He gives you a little more than you asked for. If you learn this, you can read him.
